Chapter 1 · Episodes 01–04

Changing the Mindset

Clarity comes before design, and reality is the final review.

  1. E01

    What AI Taught Me About Design Systems

    If your team's knowledge is well-documented but still hard to find, the gap usually isn't the documentation. It's the distance between how people ask and how the system expects to be asked.

  2. E02

    Enterprise UX Isn't Bigger UX

    Every step should earn its place.

  3. E03

    The Best UX Decisions Rarely Start in Figma

    Good design didn't create clarity. Clarity made good design possible.

  4. E04

    Reality Is the Final Design Review

    The final design review doesn't happen in a meeting. It happens in the real world.

Chapter 2 · Episodes 05–08

Leading Change

Simplicity and good ideas depend on what gets prioritized.

  1. E05

    The Hardest Thing to Simplify Isn't the Interface

    Transformation sometimes begins long before a user clicks anything. It begins with what they don't have to look for.

  2. E06

    Accessibility Isn't a Checklist. It's a Design Decision.

    We weren't chasing requirements. We were building capability.

  3. E07

    More Data Doesn't Always Mean More Visibility

    Don't optimize for how much data the system can show. Optimize for how people actually find what they need.

  4. E08

    Some UX Problems Aren't Design Problems. They're Prioritization Problems.

    Design can shape a good idea. Whether it ships is usually someone else's decision.

Chapter 3 · Episodes 09–12

Building Systems

A design system scales through components and stays coherent through decisions.

  1. E09

    The Problem That Led to Floret

    Floret didn't start with a component library. It started with someone deciding fragmentation was worth fixing.

  2. E10

    How a Design System Can Fail One Deadline at a Time

    Good governance doesn't wait for a complaint. It notices the drift first.

  3. E11

    Design Debt Is Just as Expensive as Technical Debt

    The fix wasn't another component. It was consolidating what we already had, then documenting how it should be used.

  4. E12

    Governance Is More Valuable Than Components

    A design system scales through components. It stays coherent through decisions.

Chapter 4 · Episodes 13–16

Designing at Scale

Consistency means knowing when to follow a rule and when to adapt it.

  1. E13

    Sometimes Consistency Is the Wrong Answer

    Designing at scale isn't about making everything the same. It's about knowing which sameness actually serves people.

  2. E14

    We Designed for the Requirement. Customers Used Something Else.

    A responsive interface can fit the screen and still miss the way people actually work.

  3. E15

    Enterprise Consistency Doesn't Kill Innovation

    Consistency isn't the opposite of personalization. It's what makes personalization safe to offer at scale.

  4. E16

    Good Design Operations Are Almost Invisible

    Good DesignOps doesn't create dependency. It creates independence.

Chapter 5 · Episodes 17–20

AI & The Future

AI amplifies whatever clarity, or confusion, you give it.

  1. E17

    AI Doesn't Fix Inconsistency. It Makes It Easier to Reproduce.

    AI doesn't fix inconsistency. It makes it easier to reproduce, faster than anyone can catch it.

  2. E18

    AI Didn't Remove the Need for Structure. It Exposed It.

    The advantage isn't having the best AI tools. It's having the clearest intent behind them.

  3. E19

    AI Generated the Right Screen for the Wrong Reality

    AI can optimize the screen you describe. It can't know the reality you forgot to mention.

  4. E20

    The Future of UX Is Designing Decisions

    The future of UX isn't choosing the best AI output. It's knowing when you don't need one.

Chapter 6 · Episodes 21–30

Reflection

Users experience the whole system. Design debt accumulates when known improvements keep getting deferred.

  1. E21

    What Enterprise UX Changed My Mind About

    Understand the system first. Then decide which screens deserve the most care.

  2. E22

    Nobody Knew Why the Setting Existed

    Better shipped beats best stalled.

  3. E23

    Same Button, Different Authority

    The step can adapt. The meaning of the action shouldn't.

  4. E24

    We Standardized the Signal, Not the Status

    Let the words stay accurate. Let the color stay consistent.

  5. E25

    We Had the Same Feature in Five Products

    Converge the experience before you converge the features.

  6. E26

    What Governance Cost Us

    Hold firm on what matters to many. Let the rest move.

  7. E27

    The Moment UX Became a Team Sport

    A simpler design lasts when every team has helped shape it.

  8. E28

    What I Would Do Differently Now

    Design for the person's whole day, not just your part of it.

  9. E29

    Most Design Debt Starts Outside Figma

    Every “later” needs an owner and a date.

  10. E30

    What Experience by Design Means to Me Now

    Design for the people who live with it every day.
